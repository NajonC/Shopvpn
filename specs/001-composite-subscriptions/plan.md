Keep authoritative money, holds, entitlement, generation, accounting history and durable work together in main SQLite. Use guarded existing Telegram receipt/catalog entry points and owner web administration, optional explicit provider capabilities, a separately supervised worker and an independent Base64 subscription server. Cache only a verified complete configuration generation; separate desired restrictions from confirmed runtime state and cleanup.

## Architecture and execution boundaries

### Local authority and financial processing

`composite/service.py` (proposed) is the common application boundary. It receives an authenticated main-tenant principal, never trusts client IDs to confer authority, and opens one transaction for each command. Compatibility `orders` rows carry `service_kind='composite'` and a unique composite checkout reference, with no fake `config_id`. Payment finalizers dispatch before legacy order claims/provision/referral logic; legacy receipt UI displays separate financial/fulfillment outcomes. `products` and `test_config_plans` discriminate the new type; corresponding selectors reject unsupported channels at server-side entry and common service layer.

Receipt submission has both FSM order linkage and `handlers_user.py`'s `db.get_latest_pending_order_awaiting_receipt(message.from_user.id)` fallback. Both dispatch composite rows before persistence/admin notification and validate tenant, acting owner and checkout/payment-attempt association. Latest pending order is not sufficient composite association evidence; ambiguous selection needs explicit owned-checkout linkage. Characterize both paths and preserve receipt uniqueness/duplicate-settlement protections (V-RCP-01).

Finalize price/tier/code/node snapshot and reserve sufficient wallet/coupon together. Main wallet mutation uses conditional sufficient balance, unique ledger effect and checked affected row; touched top-up/refund helpers combine status+credit so shared wallet paths cannot strand composite funds. Common coupon counters/holds and ordinary test holds must cover competing legacy and composite callers; a composite-only reservation table without updating affected legacy capacity checks would exceed capacity. Existing fulfilled `test_used` is preserved, unresolved holds stay separate and reset cannot erase them or permanent campaign recipients.

Verified receipt insertion, original payment role, order authorization/deadline and financial obligation/work creation are atomic. Duplicate transaction returns prior result; different actual receipt has separate late/extra role and full original quote credit. An indeterminate verification/commit stays uncertain, without release/replay. Unfunded quotes have no general TTL; valid attempts/manual review remain funding paths after new invoices are disabled. Suspension/cutover explicitly reject affected unfunded quotes. One partial unique open-renewal index covers quoted/review/authorized fulfillment; terminal money/cleanup records remain but do not alone prevent another checkout, unless accounting/entitlement is still unsafe.

### Remote effects, state and concurrency

See [data-model.md](data-model.md) and [provider contract](contracts/provider.md). Transactionally allocated operation/child/authentication identities precede calls. Each attempt stores immutable scope/input hash, desired revision, dispatch and result/uncertainty. Reads verify physical panel, canonical identity, secret fingerprint and expected attachments before mutation; no matching-name adoption. Calls apply absolute desired fields; reconcile after ambiguous response or crash before repeating. Rollback is a durable compensating operation, not a DB rollback assertion.

One worker singleton owns composite remote execution; OS panel locks plus durable ambiguous-scope barriers coordinate affected bot/API legacy provider writes. Account/role/UI requests only commit intent. Revisions reject stale local results but cannot fence already-sent calls. **RX-01**, under the existing E-001/E-002 provider capability gate, requires evidence of full prior-request completion, fencing, drain or another mechanism preventing late effects from invalidating current required state, plus current ownership/state/intent verification, before barrier release or a safe retry. Successful read-back/probes, elapsed time, client timeout, local lock release or revision checks alone are insufficient; a bounded wait requires an evidenced execution bound or other safety mechanism. No working mechanism is established here.

The conservative hold covers conflicting mutations on the physical panel, including aliases/attachments and affected legacy writers; narrow only with proven isolation/scope. Safe reads, other independent panels, local settlement and acceptance of protective intents continue. Block completion/publication only where it depends on held effects, not every existing URL automatically. Record late conflicts and observed exposure; correct them only through the safety predicate and then verify. Original deadlines/final-stage start, missed targets and escalation remain in force; a remote hold cannot postpone an otherwise processable refund. Old expiry/exhaustion disable must converge after successful eligible renewal, and obsolete enable must not undo final cancellation/independent restrictions. See V-RX-01, a source-based failure scenario, not a reproduced runtime result.

Protective requests remain admissible during checkout/other work. Leak acceptance immediately invalidates old token and records all-child revocation; reachable old nodes proceed despite other failure. New token/credentials publish only after old fresh-auth revocation, complete new readiness and Telegram retrieval. Cooldown is 86,400 seconds per subscription from successful replacement (including owner override), never failure or a fresh request. Equivalent move uses fresh moved secrets/same token, leaves unmoved identities intact, retains inactive eligibility and old exposure. Explicit emergency approval may retire old meters from serving freshness requirements; it never bypasses leak gates or claims old cleanup.

Permanent cancellation has separate acceptance/wait/final-stage timestamps. Acceptance blocks further renewal funding for that subscription. During authorized-renewal wait, pending cancellation alone neither disables existing access nor invalidates the URL; existing entitlement, expiry, quota and independent restrictions still apply. Final cancellation starts at renewal success, confirmed terminal failure or the unchanged original renewal deadline, whichever first, then invalidates/revokes and refunds that renewal even if it just succeeded. Without a required wait, fulfilled cancellation starts its final stage at acceptance. Paid initial cancellation instead immediately blocks delivery, waits only to current provisioning outcome/original deadline, then refunds/revokes. Separate normal 60-second refund/revocation targets and 15-minute escalation start at the final stage; late effects remain unresolved work, not late benefit, and credit never waits for remote safety/cleanup or notification. Unfulfilled owner/test cancellation is not a new owner product action. Desired customer disable/owner suspension/reporting hold/recovery hold remain separate and expiry continues. Account block/bot outage is separate.

### Accounting, renewal and export (conditional design)

Distinct confirmed increments are ingested once by physical meter/epoch/sample; upload/down resets are independent. Invalid boolean/negative/nonintegral values, missing identities/application errors/stale authority cannot generate samples. Under PR-002 an observation is timed at Shopvpn receipt of a valid response; an unexplained decrease opens a new epoch and records a gap, preserving confirmed totals and original grace clocks, while panel/Xray restart baseline loss is an explicit bounded store-absorbed limitation, not a gap. Delayed retired traffic is retained and attributed to the proper generation/period; old overrun never becomes debt against renewal. No remote quota reset on ordinary composite renewal.

Effective renewal commits one new period only after every child is ready under currently applicable restrictions, saving in the same transaction each meter direction's latest accepted reading as its boundary starting reading; every current-serving required reading is at most 60 seconds old at commit (PR-002). New available data is new bytes plus eligible old unspent bytes after intervening use; old overrun contributes zero debt. Pre-expiry verification is strictly `< old_expiry`, with within-window completion; at/after expiry forfeits expired data. Effective expiry is `application_time + 2,592,000 + min(max(old_expiry-application_time,0),1,296,000)` seconds. Carry old data only while eligible under FR-043/044; no separate data expiry or whole-day rounding. Term expiration during pending work still cuts old access. Coupon/reminder period changes are atomic with successful effective grant. A partially applied failed renewal refunds independently and reconciles current old terms.

**G-A stops selection of the temporal meter/billing implementation:** source lacks the evidence required above. Preserve observed intervals/ambiguity and contractual formulas; do not implement a receive-time split or silently treat uncertain interval as zero/absorbed. A generic design intention, 15-second polling or owner usage settlement cannot make normal renewal compliant. Initial/replacement/movement accounting readiness and trustworthy freshness also depend on this gate. **Resolution, 2026-10-14:** [RD-09](research.md#rd-09--renewal-boundary-attribution-from-saved-starting-readings), under owner decisions GA-OWN-001/GA-OWN-002 and the approved [PR-002](spec.md#pr-002--renewal-boundary-attribution-and-usage-observation-refinement-2026-10-14), selects receipt-timed observations, per-direction decrease epochs and saved per-meter boundary starting readings committed with the effective renewal, attributing usage by reporting order rather than per-byte time and making no remote reset or other mutation for the boundary. The receive-time-split prohibition above now means no interval is split at commit or HTTP time; reporting-order attribution at chain-node readings is the approved rule. The data model and provider accounting interface are updated against PR-002, but the stop on dependent temporal meter/billing implementation remains until the V-GA checks pass on the deployed 3x-UI v3.9.0 standalone panels with Xray-core v26.9.30.

Public read-only service performs token/generation/hold/owner-suspension gates on a fresh DB snapshot for each request, then renders cached complete real entries plus qualified info snapshots. It never calls a panel, refreshes sample time, provisions/enables or settles. Revoked token is an opaque unavailable response with no new-token redirect; uncertain generation is temporary unavailability; owner suspension returns status-only; ordinary expiry/exhaustion/customer-disable/reporting suspension/outage keeps complete current entries. See [subscription HTTP contract](contracts/subscription-http.md). The VLESS alarm-entry encoding is selected in the HTTP contract using GC-MAN-001 owner-reported five-client import/display and same-URL mixed → information-only → mixed refresh. PR-001 accepts manual or automatic selection and failed connection attempts, with no guarantee of client skipping, failover or resumption afterward. G-C is closed on GC-MAN-001 plus that explicit requirement change, unblocking the informational renderer from this gate. Automatic-selection validation is removed, not deferred; full six-node handshake/traffic/routing and all remaining E-003 checks stay required.

### Worker lifecycle and operational coordination

Proposed services `v2raybot-composite-worker` and `v2raybot-subscriptions` are required infrastructure once feature is deployed; existing `v2raybot`, `v2raybot-miniapp` and `v2raybot-adminpanel` remain. Worker discovers main DB records without requiring bot/admin availability. Start under singleton lock, verify schema/installation/restore epoch, reconcile in-flight effects, process overdue original deadlines first, then accounting/expiry/revocation/recovery/refunds/reservations/moves/reminders with reserved urgent capacity. Financial work can complete despite panel failure. Downloads survive worker/bot/optional UI failure when public infrastructure and verified cached generation remain available.

Use independent task lanes, bounded calls and per-panel fair queues; initial scheduling candidates in RD-06 are unmeasured. For remote transient work: 10-second total call timeout, at most five automatic mutation attempts at 1/2/4/8/15-second backoff where deadlines allow, each preceded by RX-01 execution-safety proof and reconciliation of uncertainty; mark missed 60-second target as soon as elapsed. Exhaustion leaves manual obligations and allows safe scheduled observation/authorized retry; no automatic deletion. Retained cleanup observations can back off to five minutes, independently of urgent current enforcement. Fifteen-minute targets/escalations refer to original acceptance/readiness/final-stage event, not last retry. Notifications bind to [N-01](contracts/operations.md#notification-delivery-policy-n-01): 5-second connect/15-second operation, five total automatic network attempts per logical notice with persistent 5/15/30/60-second backoff, and 300-second delivery opportunities. Original targets/misses and operational escalation clocks remain immutable. Event-driven plus 30-second local reevaluation automatically resumes still-relevant known-unsent reminders/overdue owner alerts using unused attempts after blocks/outages; window expiry alone is not permanent exhaustion. Accepted notices never rearm, uncertain sends do not automatically resend, and actual cap exhaustion remains visible under the bounded recovery policy; no exactly-once delivery guarantee. Backup delivery binds to [B-01](contracts/operations.md#backup-delivery-policy-b-01): 10-second connect/30-second no-progress/120-second attempt, three attempts with 15/45-second backoff bounded by capture+600 seconds and any earlier still-current rolling age deadline; new captures after an existing miss use B-01 bounded recovery without erasing the miss. Neither retry policy resets accepted clocks or capture age; both remain unmeasured under E-004.

On SIGTERM, stop accepting work, cancel/drain bounded calls, persist uncertain dispatch where needed, release locks after closing DB sessions; unexpected death leaves intents/claims, not success. No old worker can acknowledge a new restore epoch. Supervisor restart uses same operations and deadlines. Wall-clock UTC stores absolute events; monotonic timers bound waits; clock regression/large drift raises a hold for affected time decisions until trusted time is restored, without moving sold clocks. Deployment includes time-sync health and disk/DB lock/queue/sample-lag/cleanup/refund metrics with sanitized record IDs.
