**Schema design (Claude's; T040 builds exactly this):**
- Ids are TEXT, times INTEGER UTC epoch milliseconds, money INTEGER wallet units.
- A batch is saved at preview, unauthorized (`authorized_at` NULL), with its members and, for a move, each member's mapping rows. Its preview terms never change, even before authorization. Confirming sets `authorized_at` once, never earlier than `previewed_at`, and only when the batch has exactly `member_count` members and every move member has at least one mapping row. Afterwards members and mapping rows are never added, changed or removed. An unauthorized preview can be discarded (its rows deleted, children first); an authorized batch is kept.
- `action` is `owner_suspend`, `owner_resume`, `cancellation`, `move` or `compensation`. Only a move may be an emergency, and an emergency needs `emergency_acceptance_ref`. Only a compensation has `amount` (positive) and `total_amount` (= `amount × member_count`).
- A service member (every action but `compensation`) names one subscription and that subscription's customer, once per batch; at most one compensation member per customer per batch. A compensation member has no subscription; it carries the batch's `amount` and `obligation_kind` `support_compensation`, and may record `subscription_context`.
- A member's effect is linked only after authorization, once, and never changes: a service member's `operation_id` names an operation of the same subscription whose `kind` equals the member's `action`; a compensation member's `obligation_id` names a `support_compensation` obligation of the same customer and amount, used by no other member. Several batches' members may share one pending operation (R2). `result` is `pending`, then `succeeded` or `failed` (with `finished_at`, and `failure_reason` only when failed) once an effect is linked; a finished member stays finished.
- A mapping row lists one moved logical node of a move member's subscription: target binding, target descriptor, optional owner pick, and `read_at`, never later than the batch's `previewed_at`.
- T040 also adds the unique keys these foreign keys need: `composite_subscriptions (subscription_id, customer_telegram_id)` and `composite_operations (operation_id, subscription_id, kind)`.
