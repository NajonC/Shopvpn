## Rules

1. "FKs use RESTRICT for protected dependencies; no cascade deleting financial/history/cleanup." "absolute times use UTC epoch milliseconds" [data-model, storage rules] T040 adds its tables "with no cascade deletion of financial, history or cleanup rows" [tasks T040].
2. Records: "separate opportunity IDs/reasons/event/detection/start/end times, policy version, lifetime automatic network-attempt count/history, dispatch/next-attempt/acceptance/uncertain evidence and explicit recovery audit. … Persist transactional opportunity/attempt claims and never infer unused attempts from incomplete restore history." [data-model §10] "Persist … opportunity ID/start/end/reason/detection time, global attempt count/history, dispatch/outcome/evidence … Persist opportunity/attempt claims transactionally before work." [contracts/operations.md N-01, Persistence and restart]
3. Windows: "The initial opportunity ends at original due+300 seconds. A temporary eligibility/known transport block closes that opportunity without exhausting unused attempts. For a still-relevant, known-unsent notice with attempts remaining, automatically open a later opportunity when the block clears, safe processing resumes, or a deferred retry becomes due. Its end is the evidenced renewed-eligibility/processing-ready/retry-due event+300 seconds; record event and detection times, rather than silently using a late worker observation to hide lag. … If a window expires with unused attempts, mark its miss and retain `waiting_opportunity`; set `next_opportunity_at = expired_window_end + 30 seconds` … If still ready, that scheduled event opens a replacement 300-second window; it is not a new original due/eligibility event. Do not open parallel windows or shorten a later `next_attempt_at`." [N-01, Delivery opportunities]
4. Calls: "At most **five network attempts in total per logical notice** …, including any acceptance-reconciliation calls. Atomically reserve an attempt before network execution; no concurrent send for the same key. Known pre-dispatch transport failures and explicit transient no-send rejections consume an attempt … Permanent recipient/auth/payload rejection stops automatic sending immediately" [N-01, Automatic attempts] "Cancellation/timeout after dispatch is an uncertain send, not proof of rejection." [N-01, Per call] "A crash after attempt reservation with unknown dispatch outcome is held uncertain, not refunded an attempt." [quickstart V-NOT-06]
5. Uncertain sends: "Verified Telegram acceptance/message reference is terminal `accepted` for automatic sending. Lost response, ambiguous service error or crash after dispatch is `uncertain_delivery` … Reconcile through retained acceptance evidence or a supported read-only lookup if available, subject to the same per-call/300-second opportunity limits and remaining network-attempt cap; a reconciliation opportunity authorizes read-only evidence gathering, not sending" [N-01, Definitive versus uncertain]
6. Owner windows: "After genuine attempt exhaustion or permanent rejection, an explicit audited owner recovery action for a still-relevant **known-unsent** notice may authorize one 300-second window of at most two additional calls, with 30-second backoff, the same per-call limits, logical key and retained history. Each further such action is separately explicit; no automatic renewal of this manual allowance. Uncertain sends remain excluded from automatic sending unless safe nonacceptance is established. Preserve the existing separate option of an explicitly audited owner-requested send with acknowledgement of possible duplicate delivery; each send while prior delivery remains uncertain requires that explicit acknowledgement and counts against the same two-call/300-second manual allowance. Keep original uncertainty/evidence, and stop further automatic work on any new ambiguous result" [N-01, Exhaustion and later recovery]

**Schema design (Claude's; T040 builds exactly this):**
- `composite_notification_opportunities` (a window): `notification_id` required. `reason` is `original_due` (the initial window), `unblocked` (a block cleared, or an uncertain send was proven not accepted), `processing_resumed`, `retry_due`, `window_replaced` (the replacement scheduled after an expiry), `reconciliation` (lookups for an uncertain send), `owner_recovery` or `owner_duplicate_risk` (the two owner windows). `audit_event_id` is set exactly on owner windows, is unique (one owner action opens one window) and must name this notice (`resource_kind` `composite_notifications`, `resource_id` = the notice id). `event_at`, `detected_at` (≥ `event_at`) and `ends_at` (= `event_at + 300000`) are required. `closed_at` and `close_reason` are set together; `close_reason` is `expired` (the window's miss), `blocked`, `accepted`, `uncertain`, `resolved` (a lookup found evidence) or `stopped` (permanent failure, obsolete or no allowance left); `closed_at` ≥ `detected_at`, and a window closes as `expired` exactly when `closed_at` ≥ `ends_at`. Unique `(opportunity_id, notification_id)`; at most one open window and one `original_due` window per notice.
- Opening a window is checked against the notice row at that moment: `event_at` ≥ its `original_due_at`; an automatic window (`original_due`, `unblocked`, `processing_resumed`, `retry_due`, `window_replaced`) needs a known-unsent notice (`waiting_eligibility`, `waiting_transport`, `waiting_opportunity` or `sending`) with `attempts_used` < 5; `reconciliation` needs `uncertain_delivery` with `attempts_used` < 5; `owner_recovery` needs `exhausted` or `permanent_failure`; `owner_duplicate_risk` needs `uncertain_delivery`. `original_due` starts at the notice's `original_due_at`, `retry_due` at its `next_attempt_at`, `window_replaced` at its `next_opportunity_at`. A window's other columns never change; it closes once and stays closed; windows are never deleted.
- `composite_notification_attempts` (a call): foreign key `(opportunity_id, notification_id)` to its window, so a call is in a window of its own notice. `allowance` is `automatic` or `manual`; `attempt_number` is 1–5 (automatic) or 1–2 (manual); `call_kind` is `send` or `lookup`; `reserved_at` is required. `dispatched_at` is NULL or ≥ `reserved_at`. `finished_at` and `outcome` are set together, `finished_at` ≥ `reserved_at` and ≥ `dispatched_at`. `outcome` is `no_send` (definitely not sent, before or after dispatch), `accepted`, `rejected` (permanent; sends only), `uncertain` (dispatch or result unknown) or `not_accepted` (a lookup proved nonacceptance; lookups only); `accepted`, `rejected` and `not_accepted` need `dispatched_at`. `evidence_ref` is set exactly when the outcome is `accepted` or `not_accepted`.
- Reserving a call is checked against its window and notice: the window is open and `reserved_at` is in `[detected_at, ends_at)`; manual calls go exactly in owner windows and lookups exactly in `reconciliation` windows; the notice is `uncertain_delivery` for a call in a `reconciliation` or `owner_duplicate_risk` window and `sending` otherwise. An automatic call's number is the notice's `attempts_used` (the reservation update comes first), unique among the notice's automatic calls, and it is reserved no earlier than the notice's `next_attempt_at`. Manual calls are numbered 1, 2 in their window; call 2 needs call 1 finished as `no_send` at least 30 000 ms before. At most one unfinished call per notice. A call's identity columns and `reserved_at` never change, `dispatched_at` is set at most once, a finished call never changes, and calls are never deleted.
