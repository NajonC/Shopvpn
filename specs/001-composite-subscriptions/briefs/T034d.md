**Schema design (Claude's; T039 builds exactly this):**
- Ids are TEXT, times INTEGER UTC epoch milliseconds, money INTEGER shop-wallet units. Three shared tables have no `composite_` prefix: `financial_obligations`, `wallet_effects` and `resource_reservations`. The compatibility order is the legacy `orders.id`. An initial checkout has no target subscription; its subscription is found through its offer.
- A checkout's `authorization_source` is `wallet` (wallet covers the whole price), `zero_price` or `receipt` (with `original_receipt_id`); `deadline_at` = `authorized_at` + 900_000. An attempt's `create_state` is `pending`, `uncertain`, `created` (needs `provider_invoice_ref`) or `failed`; its `status` is `open`, `verified`, `expired` or `closed`. A claim's `status` is `pending`, `fulfilled`, `failed` or `uncertain`.
- An obligation is settled by a `wallet_effects` row, the ledger entry that copies its beneficiary, kind and amount; `wallet_debit` subtracts, every other kind adds.
- A reservation has exactly one holder: `checkout_id`, `claim_id`, `legacy_order_id` (legacy coupon) or `legacy_test_ref` (legacy test).
- Same-owner rules use composite foreign keys onto these unique keys: `composite_payment_attempts (attempt_id, checkout_id)`, `composite_manual_decisions (decision_id, attempt_id)`, `composite_payment_receipts (receipt_id, checkout_id)`, `wallet_effects (ledger_seq, obligation_id)`, `financial_obligations (obligation_id, beneficiary_telegram_id, kind, amount)`, `composite_test_claims (claim_id, campaign_id)` and `(claim_id, campaign_id, customer_telegram_id, status)`, and the existing `composite_plans (plan_id, kind)`.
