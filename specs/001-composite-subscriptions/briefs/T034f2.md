**Schema design (Claude's; T040 builds exactly this):**
- `composite_audit_events` has no foreign keys (its references are plain text, so it outlives what it describes). `actor_domain` is `telegram`, `web_admin`, `payment_callback`, `system` or `cli`; `actor_id`, `action`, `resource_kind`, `resource_id` are non-empty; `reason` is NULL or non-empty. A row is never updated or deleted.
- `composite_notifications`: `kind` is `fulfillment`, `failure`, `support`, `replacement`, `move`, `owner_alert` or `reminder`. Unique `(notice_key, recipient_telegram_id)`, and `reminder_id` unique. A reminder notice names its subscription and a reminder, and its `original_due_at` is that reminder's `due_at` (foreign key `(reminder_id, original_due_at)`); only a reminder notice has `reminder_id`. Only an owner alert has `escalation_ref` (required), and it names no subscription. A notice with a subscription goes to that subscription's customer. `original_target_at = original_due_at + 300000`; `original_missed_at` is NULL or ≥ the target, set once, never changed or cleared. Identity and original clocks never change.
- `status` is one of `waiting_eligibility`, `waiting_transport`, `waiting_opportunity`, `sending` (known-unsent in an open opportunity), `uncertain_delivery`, `accepted`, `permanent_failure`, `exhausted`, `obsolete`. A notice is saved as `waiting_eligibility`, `waiting_transport` or `sending` with `attempts_used` 0. `block_reason` is set exactly in the two blocked states, `next_opportunity_at` exactly in `waiting_opportunity`, `uncertain_since` exactly in `uncertain_delivery`, `accepted_at` exactly in `accepted` (with `acceptance_ref`, never before the due time), `stop_reason` exactly in `permanent_failure`, `exhausted` and `obsolete`. The three waiting states need an unused attempt; `exhausted` needs all five used. A notice accepted by its target has no miss; one accepted later has one.
- `attempts_used` (0–5) only grows, one at a time. `next_attempt_at`, once set, is never cleared or moved earlier. An `accepted` or `obsolete` row never changes again. Leaving `uncertain_delivery` for anything but `accepted` needs a new `nonacceptance_ref` in the same update. Notifications are never deleted.
- T040 also adds the unique keys these foreign keys need: `composite_subscriptions (subscription_id, customer_telegram_id)` (also needed by T034f1's tables) and `composite_reminders (reminder_id, due_at)`.
