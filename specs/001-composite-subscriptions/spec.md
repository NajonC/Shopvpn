## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Purchase and receive the complete subscription (Priority: P1)

An eligible main-store Telegram customer selects a preset, understands its shared terms and total payment cost, pays through a permitted existing method, and retrieves one complete subscription URL. The store owner can distinguish money received, provisioning, availability, and outstanding refunds.

**Why this priority**: This establishes the purchased service and protects the customer's money when delivery fails.

**Independent Test**: With prepared plans, isolated customer wallets and controlled payment/panel substitutes, execute a purchase, ambiguous creation, delivery failure, and duplicate settlement.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-01.1** — **Given** a 50-GiB/30-day six-node preset, **when** one checkout completes, **then** one customer-owned subscription has six independent credentials, one 53,687,091,200-byte shared allowance and one URL; its 720-hour term begins only when all nodes are ready and the URL is retrievable in Telegram. A second checkout creates an independent subscription.
2. **AS-01.2** — **Given** verified payment at 12:00, **when** one required node or customer URL retrieval remains unavailable at 12:15, **then** cancel fulfillment, forbid late delivery, and make the full owed wallet credit spendable by 12:16 under normal application/database conditions while retaining remote cleanup. Failed push notification alone does not cause cancellation if retrieval was available in time.
3. **AS-01.3** — **Given** price 100, eligible 10% tier and 10% code reductions, **when** checkout is confirmed, **then** the store price is 81 with unchanged service terms; forged eligibility, unauthorized approval, mismatched money and repeated callbacks cannot settle it.
4. **AS-01.4** — **Given** store price 100, actual wallet funding 30 and an accepted external invoice worth 73 wallet units including three required quoted charges, **when** full refund is owed, **then** credit 103 once using original invoice conversion even after settings/rates change. A distinct second valid invoice payment worth 73 receives its separate 73 credit, without another subscription or refund of that extra receipt.
5. **AS-01.5** — **Given** a retained unfunded quote, **when** new invoices are disabled but its existing authorized, valid, verifiable attempt or legitimate manual review remains, **then** keep the quote. **When** a configuration or payment-restriction change establishes that no permitted funding path remains, **then** reject, restore its actual wallet contribution within 60 seconds normally, release its coupon once, and credit later verified invoice money without fulfillment. Temporary outages and one expired invoice alone do not establish this condition.
6. **AS-01.6** — **Given** cancellation accepted during paid initial provisioning at 12:14 with payment verified at 12:00, **when** provisioning remains unresolved at 12:15, **then** begin final cancellation then, deliver nothing, complete the owed credit by 12:16 normally, and escalate unresolved final work at 12:30. If service was fully available before cancellation acceptance, fulfilled-service cancellation rules apply instead.

### User Story 2 - Use one allowance with truthful status and enforcement (Priority: P1)

A subscriber can choose any included connection and see shared usage and expiry. Missing observations are visible, restrictions cover every credential, and recovery never silently grants new service.

**Why this priority**: Independent credentials must honor one commercial entitlement, even when control is incomplete.

**Independent Test**: Seed a fulfilled subscription with distinct and aliased meter observations, then exercise usage, clock boundaries, panel/manager outages and recovery.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-02.1** — **Given** independent meters plus duplicate/aliased views, **when** upload/download observations repeat or arrive out of order, **then** attribute each reported underlying consumption once and preserve history through resets and other decreases, which start a new counting epoch without negative usage. Invalid, missing or stale readings never become zero or verified current usage.
2. **AS-02.2** — **Given** reconciled accounting history and every required current-serving observation trustworthy and at most 60 seconds old by Shopvpn receipt time (FR-035), **when** details or a new subscription response are fetched, **then** show reconciled usage with the oldest required observation time. At 61 seconds, or immediately upon known incomplete/untrustworthy reporting, warn of delay; a fetch cannot refresh observation time.
3. **AS-02.3** — **Given** one required meter last trustworthy at 12:00, **when** no replacement reading arrives by 12:05, **then** request suspension of the whole affected subscription, including healthy nodes. A 12:04 restart does not extend grace; known exhaustion/expiry acts earlier. Unreachable cutoff remains pending.
4. **AS-02.4** — **Given** supported normal conditions, **when** shared usage crosses its byte limit or common expiry arrives, **then** every credential denies fresh authentication within 60 seconds of that event, including detection time. Existing ordinary/multiplexed sessions are measured separately under native panel behavior; no hard outage-overrun or forced existing-session termination claim is made.
5. **AS-02.5** — **Given** all identities, accounting history, freshness and eligibility become verified at 12:00, **when** reporting suspension is the only restriction, **then** confirm automatic restoration of all required credentials by 12:01 normally, without an added dwell period. A new cancellation, expiry or independent restriction prevents stale re-enablement; unresolved recovery at 12:15 is escalated.
6. **AS-02.6** — **Given** intact entitlement/history with 50 GiB granted and 20 GiB confirmed used but an unrecoverable usage gap, **when** the owner explicitly settles the gap and current metering/eligibility are valid, **then** at most 30 GiB remains, less any recovered consumption, with original expiry and the gap still recorded as unknown. Missing purchase/revocation history cannot use this shortcut.

### User Story 3 - Renew or convert a trial without changing imports (Priority: P1)

A customer selects a currently offered matching preset, sees carryover and any forfeited time, and renews the same subscription. Only one renewal purchase is open for that subscription.

**Why this priority**: Repeat purchases must preserve paid value and restrictions without duplicate charges or grants.

**Independent Test**: Seed active, exhausted, expired, trial, disabled and owner-suspended subscriptions; test renewal with controlled usage and payment/application times.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-03.1** — **Given** 20 GiB and 10 days 12 hours remain at effective application, **when** a 50-GiB/30-day matching renewal succeeds, **then** 70 GiB is available for 40 days 12 hours through unchanged URL/credentials. With 20 old days, the result is 45 days and five old days are forfeited, disclosed before payment. Data carryover has no separate earlier expiry. Usage first reported after application, including late pre-boundary use, consumes the 70 GiB (FR-045). A late obsolete old-term cutoff is reconciled to the successfully renewed terms without clearing independent restrictions (A15; PC-036, PC-039, PC-105).
2. **AS-03.2** — **Given** expiry at 18:00, **when** payment is verified at 17:59 and renewal completes at 18:03 within its window with 20 GiB unused as reported through the 18:03 boundary starting readings, **then** grant 70 GiB and 30 days from 18:03; old access still expires at 18:00 pending renewal. Verification at 18:00 or later grants only the new allowance. A failed timely paid renewal cannot reserve expired data for another order.
3. **AS-03.3** — **Given** five of six renewal changes ready, **when** the sixth remains incomplete at the original 15-minute deadline, **then** cancel/refund that renewal once, preserve the old entitlement less actual use/time, and reconcile partial changes. The prior subscription is not deleted or replenished; old confirmed overrun creates no debt. No boundary starting reading or remote counter reset results.
4. **AS-03.4** — **Given** a renewal awaiting review or being fulfilled, **when** another renewal is requested for the same subscription, **then** return its checkout/progress with no second order, wallet debit or coupon hold. Another subscription may renew independently. After successful completion, another early renewal is allowed.
5. **AS-03.5** — **Given** owner suspension accepted while an unfunded renewal quote exists, **when** it is rejected, **then** restore holds and treat later valid money as wallet credit. A renewal already fully verified or zero-price-authorized resolves within its original window, preserving suspension and status-only downloads. Customer disable alone remains compatible with renewal and persists afterward.
6. **AS-03.6** — **Given** a fulfilled trial with 10 GiB and five days left and an eligible matching paid plan, **when** 50-GiB/30-day conversion succeeds, **then** preserve URL/credentials and grant 60 GiB for 35 days; ordinary test consumption, campaign participation and replacement cooldown stay intact.

### User Story 4 - Claim an eligible composite free test (Priority: P1)

An eligible customer uses the existing test flow to choose a legacy or composite test. The owner configures composite tests and the bounded launch campaign without creating extra per-plan eligibility.

**Why this priority**: Free tests and the campaign are accepted launch acquisition flows with scarce capacity.

**Independent Test**: Use synthetic test histories and concurrent requests at the campaign boundary; exercise failure, uncertainty, reset and conversion.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-04.1** — **Given** the common maximum is one and a legacy test has been consumed, **when** the customer requests a composite test, **then** deny another ordinary test; the converse also applies. Respect disabled-test behavior, single-plan direct delivery, multi-plan selection and active-plan checks.
2. **AS-04.2** — **Given** 149 fulfilled campaign recipients and one pending claim, **when** another eligible customer claims, **then** allocate no 151st place. Confirmed failure releases the pending customer's ordinary eligibility and campaign place once; fulfillment closes the campaign permanently at 150.
3. **AS-04.3** — **Given** a claim accepted at 12:00, **when** no complete retrievable service exists at 12:15, **then** prohibit late delivery. Unknown remote outcomes retain holds and trigger owner attention; confirmed failure releases holds. Neither creates wallet credit, and a later retry gains no inherited queue priority.
4. **AS-04.4** — **Given** a fulfilled campaign recipient, **when** ordinary test usage is reset, the trial expires/is cancelled, or it converts to paid, **then** campaign participation and permanent capacity remain unchanged. A reset previous legacy-test user who never received the campaign can qualify while places remain.
5. **AS-04.5** — **Given** unchanged quick-check defaults, **when** fulfilled, **then** grant 209,715,200 shared bytes and 3,600 seconds from availability, labeled 200 MiB. Owner edits to positive whole-hour test settings are honored; no ten-minute requirement remains. Campaign fulfillment grants 16,106,127,360 bytes for 1,296,000 seconds.

### User Story 5 - Control access and repair a leak (Priority: P1)

Paid and test customers disable/resume, replace leaked access, or permanently cancel their own subscriptions. The owner has explicit independent controls and support remedies.

**Why this priority**: Protective actions and their precedence must remain effective across payment and remote failures.

**Independent Test**: Seed fulfilled subscriptions and in-flight renewal/replacement states, then interleave authorized/unauthorized customer and owner actions.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-05.1** — **Given** a test with 40 minutes left, **when** disabled for ten minutes then validly resumed, **then** at most 30 minutes remain. Renewal, recovery and replacements retain the customer choice; customer resume cannot clear owner suspension.
2. **AS-05.2** — **Given** accepted leak replacement, **when** one old credential cannot be confirmed revoked, **then** invalidate the old URL, revoke reachable old credentials, retain pending state, and withhold the new usable URL. Never restore leaked access as fallback. Complete new readiness and old fresh-authentication revocation are required before success and cooldown start.
3. **AS-05.3** — **Given** successful replacement at 10:00, **when** another customer replacement is requested before 10:00 the next day, **then** block it for that subscription. A failed attempt consumes no allowance; a pending retry continues the same operation; an audited owner override may bypass cooldown but no other gate.
4. **AS-05.4** — **Given** permanent cancellation accepted during a renewal paid at 12:00, **when** renewal succeeds at 12:05, **then** start final cancellation immediately and, normally by 12:06, invalidate the URL, confirm all-child fresh-authentication revocation and credit the entire original renewal invoice value plus wallet funding without proration. If still unresolved, final-stage escalation is at 12:20. If renewal stalls, its original 12:15 deadline bounds the wait.
5. **AS-05.5** — **Given** ten unique customers selected for support compensation of 50 units each, **when** the owner authorizes the previewed action, **then** total credit is 500, with one 50 credit per unique customer even if selected through multiple subscriptions. Retries recover unfinished credits without repeating completed ones; service terms and test/campaign eligibility do not change.
6. **AS-05.6** — **Given** a bulk owner suspend/resume/cancel action, **when** some subscriptions complete and another fails, **then** retain per-subscription results and recovery without undoing completed results. Account blocking alone never acts as service suspension; owner suspension immediately yields status-only exports even while remote cutoff is pending.

Customers import one URL in their chosen required client and receive each configured real node plus clearly distinguished informational entries.

**Independent Test**: Validate offline complete export cases, then separately execute controlled import, refresh and traffic checks on recorded versions of all five required applications.

A panel's duplicate native addresses do not add unconfigured nodes.
2. **AS-06.2** — **Given** a fulfilled current complete configuration and panel-management outage, expiry, exhaustion, customer disable or stale-usage suspension, **when** its permitted URL is fetched, **then** retain the complete list with truthful inactive/pending and timestamped usage information. Downloads never enable, provision, renew or reset anything.

After owner release, applicable complete-list gates resume on the same URL. An invalidated URL cannot expose or redirect to replacement secrets; uncertain current-generation recovery cannot publish stale configurations.

### User Story 7 - Replace a failed or filtered server (Priority: P1)

The owner moves one or many existing subscriptions to prepared equivalent infrastructure while customers keep their subscription URLs and entitlement history.

**Why this priority**: Server continuity is required at launch, not an optional topology-editing feature.

**Independent Test**: Prepare equivalent synthetic targets and a mixed active/inactive cohort; verify individual completion, emergency authorization, sales pause and recorded capacity targets.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-07.1** — **Given** up to 150 eligible subscriptions with five moved nodes each, **when** the owner authorizes a prepared equivalent move, **then** target complete replacement availability within 15 elapsed minutes normally. Fresh moved credentials, unchanged unmoved credentials/URLs, preserved terms/restrictions and independent per-subscription outcomes are required. Larger batches are supported without this universal timing promise.
2. **AS-07.2** — **Given** old-server control unreachable, **when** the owner explicitly approves reviewed emergency cutover, **then** a fully ready replacement with trustworthy metering may serve while old revocation stays pending. Missing retired-server samples alone do not suspend it; replacement-meter staleness still does. This cannot bypass leak replacement's old-revocation gate.
3. **AS-07.3** — **Given** old topology retirement, **when** affected unfunded quotes are rejected and sales/claims pause, **then** release wallet/coupon holds once, preserve valid late-money credits, and consume no test capacity for refused requests. Already authorized work retains terms and clocks; a prepared owner-activated equivalent offering may reopen before all moves finish.
4. **AS-07.4** — **Given** 99 completed moves and one pending, **when** recovery retries, **then** do not move the 99 again; notify each completed customer to refresh independently. Expired/disabled/suspended fulfilled cases move inactive, cancelled cases remain cleanup-only, and uncertain recovery-held cases cannot bypass verification.

### User Story 8 - Recover safely and retain responsibilities (Priority: P1)

After process or host loss, the owner can distinguish verified recovery from unresolved history. Neither customer money nor revoked access is replayed from an old snapshot.

**Why this priority**: Recovery is part of the accepted commercial and operational contract.

**Independent Test**: Use disposable synthetic backups and controlled newer external evidence; exercise intact-history restart, stale restore, lost identities and protected destructive actions.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-08.1** — **Given** intact history and a manager returning at 12:30 after a 12:15 fulfillment deadline, **when** safe processing resumes, **then** no new fulfillment window starts; verifiable overdue credit becomes spendable within 60 seconds, while unknown prior financial effects remain held and overdue work is immediately visible.
2. **AS-08.2** — **Given** a snapshot predating cancellation, rotation, refund and campaign grants, **when** restored, **then** reconcile newer evidence before dependent effects, publish no unverified generation, repeat no uncertain credit, and reopen no uncertain campaign place. Release verified cases independently; irrecoverable non-usage history requires specific governed remediation.
3. **AS-08.3** — **Given** supported normal operation and host loss at 12:00, **when** recovery evidence is inspected, **then** a completed validated copy captured at 11:45 or later is accessible off the lost host. A newly delivered old copy does not satisfy this requirement.
4. **AS-08.4** — **Given** supported normal conditions, all specified recovery prerequisites ready and owner-initiated recovery at 12:00, **when** recovery runs, **then** target safe verified management, existing URL identity and accounting/recovery functions by 16:00; show held cases, prerequisite waiting, partial/overdue outcomes honestly. Retry cannot restart four hours.
5. **AS-08.5** — **Given** protected composite/campaign history or pending old-server cleanup, **when** factory reset, panel force deletion or dependent-record destruction is requested, **then** refuse loss of responsibility with an actionable explanation. Ordinary test reset remains possible without erasing campaign history.

### User Story 9 - Receive useful paid-service reminders (Priority: P2)

A paid customer receives one logical expiry reminder and one quota reminder per paid period when renewal is eligible; the owner controls the existing valid thresholds.

**Why this priority**: Reminders support continuity but cannot change enforcement or financial outcomes.

**Independent Test**: Seed paid reminder periods with carried data and restricted states; exercise boundaries and delivery failures without real messages.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **AS-09.1** — **Given** default settings and eligible paid service, **when** remaining time enters 120 hours or current-period use reaches 80%, **then** target the corresponding Telegram acceptance within five minutes normally, once per type per period. In selected remaining-data mode use its configured binary threshold, default two GiB; do not send twice for both quota conditions.
2. **AS-09.2** — **Given** owner suspension, uncertainty or movement blocking renewal, **when** a threshold is met, **then** wait without consuming the notice and reevaluate after release. Customer disable alone permits reminders. Still-free trials receive no optional reminders, and no reminder creates a coupon or claims current connection status.
3. **AS-09.3** — **Given** successful notice delivery, **when** settings toggle, refresh, restart or replacement occurs, **then** do not rearm it. Successful renewal/conversion starts a new period using resulting allowance, not lifetime consumption; failed delivery remains recoverable and is not marked sent merely because attempted.

### Edge Cases

- **EC-01**: Repeated callbacks, simultaneous Telegram/web approval, payment reused on another order, and remote success followed by timeout/local write failure must not multiply money, credentials, grants, coupon uses or test reservations (FR-011, FR-014–FR-022).
- **EC-02**: Accepted quote edits, later code expiry, invoice expiry, disabled new invoices, legitimate pending manual review and a truly unpayable remainder have different outcomes; quote retention never freezes usage or future attempt conversion rates (FR-012–FR-017).
- **EC-03**: Known exhaustion with another stale meter acts immediately under cutoff policy; no five-minute grace is added. Healthy 60-second freshness and 60-second cutoff are separate targets, not sequential allowances (FR-033–FR-039).
- **EC-04**: Sample decreases, independent upload/download resets, shared attachments, aliases, overlapping samples and a retired credential's delayed traffic preserve distinct consumption evidence and uncertainty; decreases start new counting epochs without negative usage. Collector restart loss is a bounded store-absorbed limitation, not an unknown gap, and usage first reported after a renewal boundary follows FR-045 (FR-033–FR-045; PR-002).
- **EC-05**: Payment verified strictly before expiry differs from at expiry; fractional remaining days and daylight-saving changes must not alter elapsed-time arithmetic (FR-003, FR-043–FR-044).
- **EC-06**: Cancellation during verified renewal follows its bounded sequential rule; cancellation during paid initial provisioning immediately blocks delivery while waiting. Any other overlapping work must converge on accepted permanent restrictions without re-enabling stale credentials; technical coordination cannot silently reject promised protective controls (FR-026, FR-055–FR-062, FR-085).
- **EC-07**: Old-server emergency exposure is not proof of cutoff and not a leak-repair exemption. Inactive subscriptions may move without becoming active; cancelled/uncertain histories cannot be recreated (FR-065–FR-071).
- **EC-08**: Trial reservation uncertainty beyond 15 minutes holds capacity but prohibits late fulfillment. Coupon, wallet, ordinary test usage and permanent campaign records are separate obligations (FR-020–FR-032).
- **EC-09**: A blocked account, blocked bot, failed notification, disabled optional UI or overdue backup alone does not revoke VPN access or erase financial obligations (FR-052, FR-063, FR-072, FR-076).
- **EC-10**: An outdated restore may lack newer purchases, revocations, rotations, receipts, coupon uses, claims or panel children. Same-epoch counters cannot reconstruct every missing fact, and local success/absence alone cannot establish remote cleanup (FR-073–FR-077, FR-083).
- **EC-11**: Native panel links may have duplicates or incomplete fields. Informational entries may be selected manually or automatically and fail to connect; client skipping/failover/resumption is not guaranteed and automatic-selection investigation is not required (PR-001). Neither a parseable list nor screenshot appearance proves real-node compatibility (FR-049–FR-051, FR-086).

#### PR-001 — Informational-entry selection refinement (2026-10-10)

**Authority:** Explicit owner instruction on 2026-10-10 refines the informational-entry selection obligations associated with PC-077 and PC-080 and their historical E-003 investigation wording. Those accepted-decision records retain their historical wording; this later refinement controls the current requirement.

Informational entries may be selected manually or automatically, and resulting connection attempts may fail. Client automatic skipping, failover and automatic resumption after such selection are outside the product guarantees. Remove the requirement to investigate or validate informational-entry automatic-selection behavior in each client; it is neither a pending gate nor deferred release/checklist work. No unselectability or custom client is required.

Import/display, same-URL mixed → information-only → mixed refresh, all intended real-node functionality, truthful expiry/quota/freshness/restriction text, secrecy and unrelated validation remain required. This refinement concerns client selection only; it does not alter store-managed service/access recovery, notification recovery or any other automatic-resumption obligation. FR-050, EC-11, SC-008 and E-003 below reflect this change. It records an explicit requirement change, not evidence that automatic-selection testing occurred.

#### PR-002 — Renewal-boundary attribution and usage-observation refinement (2026-10-14)

**Authority:** Explicit owner decisions on 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14 refine the usage-observation, counter-reset and renewal-boundary obligations associated with PC-004, PC-027, PC-034, PC-035, PC-038, PC-079, PC-084 and PC-115 and the earlier boundary-attribution design obligation. Those accepted-decision records retain their historical wording; this later refinement controls the current requirement.

A usage observation is a successful, application-valid traffic response from the verified meter authority for an owned identity, timed when Shopvpn received it; no separate panel source clock or collector-liveness proof is required. Ordinary renewal and conversion never reset remote traffic counters. At the effective renewal boundary Shopvpn saves each meter direction's latest accepted reading. Old-period usage is usage reported through those readings; new-period usage is later readings minus the saved readings. Known eligible unused data carries over and old overrun reported before the boundary is absorbed. Usage first reported after the boundary counts against the renewed allowance, even if part occurred shortly before it within normal panel reporting delay; exact per-byte attribution to the boundary instant is not required. A customer renewing while exhausted can therefore lose to the renewed allowance about the last collection interval's pre-boundary usage; otherwise the result is unchanged.

Consumption the panel collector discards after a panel/Xray restart is a bounded, explicitly recorded measurement limitation absorbed by the store, not an unknown usage gap. An undetected collector stall while VPN service continues is unmetered exposure under best-effort cutoff. Composite children are not intentionally reset or configured for panel periodic traffic reset; any counter decrease still starts a new epoch, preserves confirmed usage, never yields negative usage and remains explicit uncertainty. Modifying 3x-UI or Xray remains out of scope.

FR-034, FR-035, FR-037, FR-040, FR-043–FR-046, AS-02.1, AS-02.2, AS-03.1–AS-03.3, EC-04, SC-003, SC-005, E-001, E-002, the timing table, NG-10, Key Entities and Assumptions reflect this change. It records an explicit requirement change, not evidence that deployed panels behave as described; E-001/E-002 controlled checks remain required.

### Functional Requirements

#### Offer, topology and authority

- **FR-001 — One entitlement**: Each composite MUST bind one authenticated customer, shared finite allowance, validity policy and URL to an arbitrary configured collection of independent 3x-UI child credentials. Same-panel/same-inbound children are supported with distinct authentication and intended routing identities. Children, exported nodes, purchased units and underlying meters MUST remain distinct concepts; child count MUST NOT multiply allowance. VLESS/VMess credentials use UUIDs and Trojan credentials use protocol passwords. (PC-001–PC-004)
- **FR-002 — Required deployment fixtures**: Support the six-node fixture below without a six-node limit, and the initial live A-only offer with five explicitly configured choices/independently routed credentials on A's prepared inbound. B/C need not be live launch servers, but multi-panel acceptance remains required. Five outbounds alone are not five exported nodes or independent entry-IP failure domains. A MUST retain one public REALITY inbound on port 443 in the described Steal-Oneself deployment; add no public VPN listeners or L4 proxy for this feature. (PC-002, PC-085)
- **FR-003 — Presets and exact units**: Customers MUST choose owner-defined presets and cannot override nodes, allowance or duration. Checkout MUST present the selected node choices, shared allowance, duration and base price, followed by applicable reductions and total payable cost under FR-009–FR-013. All launch composite plans have finite positive data and time; legacy unlimited sentinels MUST NOT create unlimited composites. Paid offers advertise exactly “30 days”: 720 elapsed hours / 2,592,000 seconds. The renewal cap is 45 elapsed days: 1,080 hours / 3,888,000 seconds. No calendar-month, midnight, DST or whole-day carryover rounding changes these terms. Paid allowances use explicit GiB labels in customer/owner views, with a brief binary-unit explanation: one GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes. Test exceptions are FR-028–FR-029. Third-party labels cannot alter the purchased bytes. (PC-008, PC-015–PC-016, PC-030–PC-033)
- **FR-004 — Prepared infrastructure**: The store owner MUST prepare shared listeners/inbounds, outbounds and routing policy. Shopvpn manages subscription identities and intended route assignments within that setup. Lifecycle operations MUST preserve unrelated routes, avoid cloning country outbounds or rewriting shared routing, and verify intended egress rather than infer it from flags. Shared configuration generation/editing is deferred; no routing syntax/mechanism is selected. (PC-083)
- **FR-005 — Approved channels and roles**: Customer purchase, test, retrieval and approved self-service MUST use the main-store Telegram bot. Composite plan/test-plan configuration and manual subscription management MUST be main-store-owner-only through web admin. Server-side checks MUST reject unsupported Mini App/reseller-store/supply/direct endpoint paths and unauthorized staff/customer actions, rather than merely hide controls. Manual payment decisions are the explicit two-interface exception in FR-014. Automated ordinary processing MUST NOT require the owner online. (PC-006–PC-010, PC-093)
- **FR-006 — Buyer and ownership**: Otherwise eligible main-store buyers MUST NOT be excluded merely for reseller/discount status. They buy as ordinary main-store customers without reseller GB credit, fixed inventory or cross-tenant ownership. Purchase/claim assigns the authenticated acting account; renewal, conversion and either replacement retain it. Knowing/sharing a URL confers no account management right. Refunds and extra credits go to the verified checkout beneficiary. Applicable blocked-account and ownership checks remain effective. (PC-050, PC-101)
- **FR-007 — Customer and owner visibility**: Authorized customers MUST be able to inspect their own subscription terms, current restrictions, qualified usage, checkout/fulfillment/refund/replacement progress and support path in Telegram. The owner MUST see affected subscriptions, individual child pending/failure states and financial/cleanup obligations needed for approved operations. Status MUST distinguish requested, partial, uncertain and confirmed outcomes. No staff read access or general history-browser feature is implied. (PC-007, PC-010–PC-014, PC-046, PC-067, PC-075, PC-119)
- **FR-008 — Quantity and saved offer**: Each initial checkout MUST create exactly one subscription; separate purchases remain independent in URL, credentials, allowance, time and financial outcome. Renewal targets one subscription. Quantity discounts use quantity one, not child count or aggregated orders. Sold/issued terms and intended nodes MUST survive later catalog/test-plan edits; explicit equivalent movement is separately governed. ### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

These are domain concepts, not table or API designs.

- **Composite plan / saved offer**: Owner-defined intended node choices/order/remarks, shared allowance, duration, base price and applicable restrictions; accepted quotes and issued subscriptions retain agreed terms independently of later edits.
- **Subscription / subscription owner**: One paid or test entitlement managed by its authenticated buying/claiming customer, with URL identity, common expiry, usage/history, independent restrictions and separate operations. Store owner is a distinct operator role.
- **Child credential / node / inbound / outbound**: Independently managed authentication identity; intended exported real configuration; listener scoped to a panel; and shared prepared egress. Their counts/identities are not interchangeable.
- **Underlying meter and observation**: Owned consumption source, trustworthy observations timed at receipt of valid panel responses and accrued evidence across resets/generations; counter decreases start new counting epochs, renewal boundaries save per-meter, per-direction starting readings, and duplicates/aliases do not create new consumption. Unknown historical gaps and the bounded collector restart limitation remain explicit (PR-002).
- **Confirmed checkout quote / payment attempt quote**: Retained store offer versus one attempt's native payable cost, charges, conversion/rounding and original wallet value. One renewal checkout can have several valid attempts but settles its purchase once.
- **Financial obligation**: Original wallet funding, valid invoice receipt, owed full refund, late/extra credit or discretionary compensation, with beneficiary, evidence and pending/confirmed outcome. Cleanup is independent.
- **Coupon use / ordinary test eligibility / campaign claim**: Separate capacity concepts with holds, terminal releases and consumption rules; campaign fulfillment establishes permanent participation.
- **Restriction / lifecycle operation**: Customer disable, owner suspension, stale-usage suspension, cancellation, recovery hold, renewal, leak replacement or equivalent move; desired state differs from confirmed remote state and each retains relevant history/deadlines.
- **Retired identity / cleanup responsibility**: Old credential and meter evidence still required for revocation, exposure accounting and reconciliation despite refund, move, cancellation or restore.
- **Information entry / paid reminder period**: Non-working imported status snapshot versus current paid-term reminder context; neither creates a credential, allowance or proof of live connection.
- **Recovery point / recovery case**: Validated available historical local state and its capture time, plus verification of newer external facts, unresolved gaps, independently releasable operations and governed remediation.
